# Store listing draft

App name (23/30): **Daybook – Voice Journal**

Short description (under 80 characters):
Capture your day with offline voice notes, photos, goals and reflections.

Full description:

Daybook gives everyday moments a place to stay. Record a thought, write a note, or attach a photo, then return to a chronological journal of your day.

VOICE, EVEN OFFLINE
Choose Whisper base.en, Moonshine v2 Small/Medium, or Parakeet TDT v2 in Settings and download its English model once. Transcribe saved voice notes without an internet connection. Whisper also offers optional live draft text. Pause, resume, or discard a recording, mark moments with audio bookmarks, and tap saved transcript segments to hear the original audio. Recording can continue with the screen locked, with a notification to stop and save. Each note supports up to 30 minutes of captured audio.

YOUR ORIGINALS, PRESERVED
Keep original audio, photos, note text, edits, and review history. Browse by day, search locally, or use the calendar and statistics. Recently Deleted keeps notes recoverable for 30 days before background maintenance removes them.

GOALS, TASKS AND IDEAS
Create daily, weekly or one-time goals with checkboxes or numeric targets. Log partial effort and see how many distinct goals you worked on. Keep to-dos and ideas in separate lists. AI suggestions require your approval before they create goals or change progress.

REFLECT YOUR WAY
Choose on-device text reflections after a separate model download, or optional OpenAI reviews using your own API key. OpenAI can review eligible attached photos; the local model reviews text only. Keep selected entries on-device and choose whether a note's photos can be included in future cloud reviews. Manual cloud reflections previews its input. Weekly reflections bring together journal moments and confirmed activity.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose light or dark themes, approximate reminders, quiet hours, optional biometric/device-credential lock, reduced animations, and compact dark home-screen capture widgets.

BACK UP YOUR MEMORIES
Export passphrase-encrypted full backups with audio and photos, or enable daily backups to your chosen folder. Restore on another phone using the passphrase. API keys and downloaded models are excluded. Markdown exports provide readable journal text.

No Daybook account or developer-operated journal server is required. No ads or analytics SDKs are included. English speech needs an approximately 141 MB download; optional local reviews need approximately 1.1 GB and additional working memory. Capture and manual tracking work without either model. Optional OpenAI use requires internet and is billed by OpenAI to your API account.

AI text and calorie suggestions can be inaccurate and require review. Daybook is a personal journal and goal tracker; it does not diagnose, treat, or provide medical advice. Android battery settings can delay reminders and background processing.
