Daybook Privacy Policy
Updated: October 21, 2026

Daybook is a personal journal. It has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or developer-operated journal server.

ON YOUR PHONE
Journal entries, audio, photos, transcripts, revisions, goals, activity, to-dos, ideas, weekly reflections, and review history are stored in private app storage. Android provides app isolation and device encryption. The database is not separately encrypted by Daybook. Optional app lock protects interactive access and blocks screenshots; configured background recording and reflections can continue.

LOCAL MODELS
English speech transcription runs on-device with the selected Whisper, Moonshine v2, or Parakeet TDT v2 model. Optional on-device text reviews use Qwen 2.5 1.5B through llama.cpp. Models are downloaded from Hugging Face or download.moonshine.ai over HTTPS and verified with pinned SHA-256 checksums before use. Runtime libraries are bundled with the app. Model hosts and their delivery providers receive ordinary connection information, such as IP address, when downloading. Journal content is not included in model downloads. After download, local inference does not require a network or API key. Local reviews do not analyze photos.

OPTIONAL OPENAI REVIEWS
Only when you choose OpenAI and enable cloud consent, the app sends eligible current note text, selected attached photos, active goals, confirmed daily activity, and open to-do/idea titles directly to OpenAI using your own API key. Original audio is never included. ‘Keep this entry on-device’ excludes that entry and its photos from future OpenAI requests. Photo inclusion can be disabled separately. Derived activity and list context with private source references are excluded; independently entered goal/list titles remain context. Existing requests cannot be retracted.

Manual reflections preview this input. Automatic nightly reflections use your saved choices and cloud consent. Your API key is encrypted using Android Keystore and is not included in backups or diagnostics. Requests use store: false. This does not guarantee zero retention; OpenAI retention and abuse-monitoring rules may apply. See https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data.

Generated reviews and suggestions are separate from original notes. Suggestions may be inaccurate. Goal progress, new goals, tasks, ideas, and calorie estimates require user confirmation.

BACKUPS AND DELETION
Android automatic backup and transfer are disabled. Optional full backups are encrypted with your passphrase and include original media, Recently Deleted, and weekly reflections. They exclude API credentials, downloaded models, and the saved automatic-backup passphrase. Automatic backups write to a folder you choose and keep the seven latest tracked successful backups. A cloud document provider may sync the encrypted files under its own terms.

For automatic backups, you explicitly choose to remember the passphrase encrypted with Android Keystore. Disabling automatic backups removes that saved secret. Keep the passphrase separately; uninstalling or moving phones can make the local saved secret unavailable.

Saved notes and their media are hidden in Recently Deleted for 30 days after deletion, then removed when background maintenance runs. You can restore or permanently delete earlier. Confirmed goal activity remains. Uninstalling removes the app's local data. Exported files, user-created backups, cloud-provider copies, and data already transmitted to OpenAI are independent copies; deleting a note does not remove those copies.

Discarding an unfinished recording removes that recording’s audio, live draft, and draft revisions immediately after capture stops. It does not move them to Recently Deleted or change earlier entries and reflections.

FEEDBACK
Feedback is a user-reviewed draft shared only through an app you choose. Optional diagnostic details show app version, Android/device model, free space, and model readiness. The app does not automatically send logs, notes, audio, photos, API keys, paths, or device identifiers. Review your own feedback text before sharing.

PERMISSIONS
Microphone access is used for user-started recording, with a foreground notification and stop controls. Notifications support reminders and recording controls. Photos use Android's picker or a user-invoked camera app. A selected backup folder uses Android's document permission. Biometric/device credentials are handled by Android.

CONTACT AND CHANGES
The public release identifies the developer, support contact, and public policy link in Settings → Privacy policy & feedback. Local beta builds without those configured values display their beta status. This policy will be updated when data handling changes.
